# Citing LOCOMgcm

When citing the software, include **LOCOMgcm**, the repository or archive
URL, and the Git commit or release tag used in your work.

The associated article is **LOCOMgcm: A Differentiable, Fast Ocean General
Circulation Model in PyTorch**. Use the authors and bibliographic details of
the published article when citing it.

For MITgcm comparisons, also cite the upstream MITgcm revision used by the
case. Copyright and license notices for MITgcm-derived code are retained in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
